01 / ABOUT

More than pixels & components.

I'm Pranjal Jain, a Frontend Engineer with 5+ years of experience building enterprise web applications and data-driven interfaces.

My core stack is React.js, TypeScript and JavaScript. I enjoy solving the messy frontend problems: complex workflows, dynamic forms, editable tables, API-driven interfaces, reusable component systems, accessibility and performance.

Currently at Infosys, I serve as a UI Lead on a Ford engagement, working with client-side teams and coordinating a 5–6 member UI team. I also build AI-powered frontend workflows and interactive UI patterns that fit naturally into existing applications.

Outside enterprise work, I'm co-building Muhurat.in with my husband, Nikhil Jain — an independent product exploring personalized, game-based and story-driven digital experiences.
